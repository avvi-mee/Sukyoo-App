they provide.

## Emergency SOS — read this

The Emergency SOS feature notifies your linked family members and Sukyoo's
care-coordination team when you trigger it, and can help arrange ambulance
dispatch through our logistics partners. **It is not a substitute for
dialing your local emergency number (112 in India) directly, and delivery
of SOS alerts depends on your device having network connectivity and
notification permissions enabled.** If you are in immediate danger, call
your local emergency number first.

You must provide accurate information to register. Family linking uses a
one-time invite code you generate and share yourself — you're responsible
for who you share that code with. You control what data categories a linked
family member can access, and can revoke access at any time.

responsible for medical advice given during a consultation. Vitals-tracking
and medication-reminder features are informational aids, not medical
devices, and don't replace your doctor's guidance.

in-app permission controls, you grant that family member the ability to
view/listen to that content per the access level you choose.
